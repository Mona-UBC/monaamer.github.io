About Me

I am a mechanical engineer doing research on wind energy in seismic-prone areas to develop suitable structural health monitoring concepts that will contribute to future digital twins in this area. By understanding the dynamic behavior of critical infrastructure, we intend to contribute to its expansion in hazardous locations with ideal operating condition such as the Pacific Coast line.

I am strongly interested in interdisciplinary research bridging the gap between purely technical disciplines and social sciences to reach solutions which have a long-lasting, positive impact on the community.

I view enducation as the key to tackle society's most pressing challenges. With my approach I am hoping to empower future engineers not only to be technically proficient but also be aware of their responsibilities and ethical decision making.

Research Interests

  • Wind Energy & Structural Dynamics: Developing novel structural identification techniques for operating wind turbines in remote locations.
  • Socio-Technical Engineering Education: Integrating ethical considerations, sustainability, and societal impact into core technical curricula.
  • Community energy: Supporting the objective of remote communities and First Nations for energy sovereignty by researching community-based energy approaches and participation in the energy transition.

Teaching Philosophy

I am passionate about educating the next generation of engineers - ready to meet the challenges of tomorrow as critical thinkers while being aware of their responsibility and centering sustainability.

My teaching follows an active learning approach with in-class engagement and team work components. I leverage my background as a certified organizational coach for inquiry-based learning, using open-ended questioning to foster critical thinking and socio-technical competencies in students sparking intrinsic motivation and curiosity.

I believe effective learning is built on transparency, authenticity, trust and communication. By implementing student feedback in real time, I strive to make student voices count and to create an inclusive learning environment where all students feel empowered to engage with complex engineering concepts.
