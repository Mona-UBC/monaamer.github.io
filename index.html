Research Interests

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach centers on active learning, mutual trust, and psychological safety. I leverage my background as a certified organizational coach to facilitate inquiry-based learning, using open-ended questioning to spark intrinsic motivation and curiosity.

I believe effective learning is built on transparency and responsiveness. By implementing real-time feedback loops and accessible course materials, I strive to create an inclusive environment where all students feel empowered to engage deeply with complex engineering concepts.
